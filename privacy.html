Hold'em Trainer — Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026
In short: Hold'em Trainer has no accounts and doesn't collect your name, email or other personal details. Your hands, stats, achievements and settings are stored on your device. The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used (which screens are opened, hands played, and similar events), and shows ads from Google AdMob — both described below.

This policy covers the Hold'em Trainer app for Android and the Hold'em Trainer website (together, "the app"). Hold'em Trainer is a strategy trainer for casino table games. It involves no real-money play.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, using your phone's or browser's local storage:

  • your settings (pay tables, sound, what you can see before the flop);
  • your practice stats, daily challenge results, casino simulator bankroll and trips, and achievements;
  • your hand history, which the Leak finder uses to spot patterns in your play;
  • calculation results the app saves to work faster next time.

This information itself (your hand history, stats and settings) is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete your hand history in Settings, and uninstalling the app (or clearing your browser's data for the website) removes all of it.

Usage analytics (Android app only)

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, so we can improve it. It records events such as which screens you open, practice hands and daily challenges completed (with how close to perfect play they were), casino trips started and finished, range charts viewed, achievements earned, and whether ad-related buttons were used. These events are tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name or account, and include general device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, country) that Google Analytics collects automatically.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, analytics storage follows the choice you make in Google's consent form. Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. The website doesn't use analytics.

Advertising (Android app only)

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen ads, and optional videos you can choose to watch. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

The website shows no ads.

Purchases (Android app only)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device makes a request to Google's servers when the app starts. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Sharing

If you use "Share" on a daily challenge result, your phone's share sheet opens with a short text summary. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with.

Children

The app is a simulated casino-games trainer and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
